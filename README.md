## About Minibikemania

**Minibikemania** is a **Barbershop** in **Chicago** — Book or walk in—clean fades, hot towels optional, no rush jobs.

We publish hours, offerings and contact paths clearly so customers know what to expect before they visit or book.

## What drives us

Minibikemania is a barbershop built around real neighbors in Chicago. We'd rather do fewer things well than overwhelm you with noise.

- Curated offerings over endless catalogs
- Human replies instead of auto-responses
- Seasonal updates that actually change
- A front door that's easy to find

## Looking ahead

Expand what Minibikemania does best while keeping the same welcome — evolving with beauty trends without losing our local voice.
